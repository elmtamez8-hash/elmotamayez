# Gotchas — Exams, questions, practice and grading

Moved verbatim from `CLAUDE.md` (the rule headlines stay there). Read this file before changing `Modules/Assessments/`, exams, the question bank, practice, study rooms. Bullets that say «above» may now point into another file in `docs/gotchas/`.

- **`concept_stats.lesson_id` is `NOT NULL` and zero means "the concept overall".** NULL never equals NULL, so a unique index carrying a nullable column does not bite on the one row every screen reads: `upsert()` matches nothing and INSERTs, a new row every night, and after a month the page shows whichever came back first — a number thirty days old while the correct one sits beside it, with not one error logged. `RollupIdempotencyTest` runs the job TWICE for that reason; one run is green for ever and proves the opposite. The rollup also excludes two families of row that are invisible in a naive `COUNT`: `is_practice` attempts (the flag is on `exam_attempts` while the answers are on `exam_answers`, so the join exists only for it), and essays with `requires_grading` and no `graded_at` — `GradeAttempt` writes those `is_correct = false` because no machine can judge them, so counting them reports every essay in the bank as 100% wrong, permanently. And a sample below `assessments.min_sample_size` stores `wrong_pct = NULL`, never zero: "nobody got this wrong" is what makes a teacher delete a good question two students happened to sit.
- **The snapshot is the denominator, and `attempt_items` is written at START.** Grading reads the snapshot, never the live question, so an exam edited after a student sat it cannot change what they were marked on. Two consequences that are not obvious: a migration had to BUILD those rows for every pre-existing attempt or each one's denominator is zero — and `SC-015` passes blind, because it measures `score` alone — and `GradeAttempt` writes an answer row for **every** item rather than for the answered ones, since a question left blank is the strongest evidence of a gap and would otherwise be missing from the mistake notebook entirely.
- **A question carries EXACTLY ONE correct option, and «at least one» was the rule until a teacher asked why two was allowed.** `GradeAttempt::matchesSnapshot()` compares the correct SET against the selected SET, and every answering surface in this product is single-select — so a second correct option is a question one tap can never satisfy: every student wrong for ever, and a `wrong_pct` of 100% that reads as the hardest item in the bank, which is the number a teacher deletes a good question over. **It is the same defect as NO correct option, reached from the opposite side**, which is why one rule refuses both. Guarded in `SaveQuestionRequest` and again in `SaveQuestion::guardExactlyOneCorrect()` — the importer, the seeder and the panel reach `syncOptions` with no form behind them — and named separately in `ImportQuestions`, because a per-row reason is read beside a row number and must say which COLUMN to edit. The three types are `mcq`, `true_false` and `essay`; none of them means "choose all that apply", and one that did would need its own type, its own student screen and its own partial-credit rule. The two answering surfaces (`PracticeRunner.tsx`, `exams/[uuid]/take/page.tsx`) replace the selection rather than accumulating it — they used to add, so a second tap silently turned a right answer into a zero on a graded paper.
- **The practice pool subtracts the exam not yet sat, and the notebook deliberately does NOT filter practice.** One bank question serves several exams, so a question missed last month can also be in next week's paper: practise it and the platform marks it instantly and shows the explanation, which is the exam handed over a week early. The mistake notebook is the mirror image — answering correctly IN a practice run is exactly how a mistake gets fixed, so filtering practice out means the "test me on my mistakes" loop can never resolve anything and returns the same questions for ever.
- **`claimForGrading()` outside the transaction strands the attempt for ever, and the refusal must stand BEFORE the claim.** `Attempt::claimForGrading()` is one conditional UPDATE moving `in_progress → grading`, and **nothing in the tree moves it back**. So a throw after it — a duplicate answer set colliding on `unique(attempt_id, question_id)`, a 500 anywhere in the loop — leaves the paper permanently unmarkable, with no sweep that notices and no screen that explains. `GradeAttempt` therefore refuses an attempt that already carries answer rows *above* the claim: refusing after it strands the attempt in exactly the state the refusal exists to prevent. The claim itself must still come before any write — reading a status and then acting on it is the race two taps win together, and a duplicated answer set double-counts every mistake in the notebook and doubles the denominator of every `wrong_pct`.
- **`PracticePool::withheldQuestionIds()` is computed PER STUDENT, so a room's door is «my own pool contains every question in it», never «I am enrolled here».** A host who has legitimately sat a published exam freezes its questions into their study room; every joiner who has *not* sat it then answers them and is handed the correct option ids and the explanation on the spot — next week's paper with its answers, which is precisely what the pool exists to prevent. The weaker predicate opens a second hole in the same line: `questionsFor()` is bounded by the student's own COURSES, not by the workspace, so a student enrolled in «العربي» would join a room built from the «الفيزياء» bank at the same teacher. `StudyRoomAccess::mayJoin()` counts the frozen questions this student may reach and compares against `study_rooms.question_count` — which is why that column stores what was actually DELIVERED and not what was asked for, and why the check is one query instead of a fetched id list counted in PHP.
- **The course certificate issues on `CourseCompleted` ALONE, and an exam reaches it only by completing its item (owner decision, 2026-09-25).** `ExamPassed` used to issue the whole-course certificate directly, so a week-one quiz passed at 10% progress certified the course. Removing that was safe only once a course whose LAST item is an exam still completes on its pass — which needed two things. **`CompleteExamLessonOnSubmission` listens to `ExamPassed` as well as `ExamSubmitted`**: an essay paper submits as `pending_grading`, so a pass-gated item is unmet at submission and the pass arrives later, when a person marks it. **And it is `ShouldQueueAfterCommit`, not `ShouldQueue` + `ShouldHandleEventsAfterCommit`** — for a QUEUED listener `Illuminate\Events\Dispatcher` never reads the second interface (the queued branch is taken before the after-commit wrapper, and only `ShouldQueueAfterCommit` sets the job's `afterCommit`), so the job was pushed from inside `GradeAttempt`'s transaction and read `passed = false`. Historical `exam_passed` certificates are left standing. ⚠️ **Thirty-six other classes in `app/` carried the same `ShouldQueue` + `ShouldHandleEventsAfterCommit` pair and believed it deferred them; all are `ShouldQueueAfterCommit` now, and `tests/Feature/Architecture/QueuedListenersAfterCommitTest.php` fails the build over the pair.** ⚠️ **That guard cannot see a BARE `ShouldQueue` whose event is raised inside a transaction**, and four were (2026-09-25): `NotifyTeacherApproved` / `NotifyTeacherRejected` (raised inside `ApproveTeacherApplication` / `RejectTeacherApplication`'s transactions), and `NotifyStudentGradingPending` / `AwardOnMistakeResolved` (raised from `GradeAttempt::grade()`, which runs under `DB::transaction()` — the second through `AnswerMarker`). A rollback still told a teacher they were approved and paid points for an answer row that was never written. All four are `ShouldQueueAfterCommit` now, measured by `RolledBackEventQueuesNothingTest`; a new queued listener whose event can fire inside a transaction belongs in that test's dataset.
- **Starting an exam asks the course's sequence, and the attempt allowance is a claim.** `StartAttempt` refuses an official attempt by an enrolled non-author while every visible placement of the exam in their course is locked by `LessonGate::for()` — reused, never re-derived; a draft placement gates nothing; the author exemption is the pivot role in the EXAM's workspace, not `teachesOnPlatform()`. The count of used attempts is only a pre-check: `unique(exam_id, student_user_id, attempt_number)` (hand-named `exam_attempts_attempt_number_unique`) is what lets exactly one of two double-tapped starts have the number, and the number is `max(count, highest) + 1` so a deleted attempt never makes the next start collide for ever. Practice runs carry `NULL` and never collide. A sequential «start twice» test passes with no claim at all; `AttemptLimitRaceTest` writes the competitor from a query listener on the count.
- **Any code that deletes `exam_answers` must call `QuestionStatRollupState::requestFullRecompute()` — or the nightly item analysis keeps counting answers that no longer exist.** Since 2026-09-27 `RollUpQuestionStatsJob` is incremental: it recomputes only the questions with an answer whose `updated_at` is past the last run's watermark. A DELETE leaves no `updated_at` for it to find, so a question whose answers the retention sweep or an erasure removed keeps its old `wrong_pct` and sample size for ever, with nothing logged — and `RollupIncrementalEqualsFullTest` cannot see it: its deletion case calls `requestFullRecompute()` itself, so it proves the flag works, never that a new delete raises it. `AssessmentsPersonalData::answersDeleted()` is that module's own door to the call, and every delete there goes through it; a new delete anywhere else must make the same call in the same change. ⚠️ **The rule covers an `exam_attempts` row that carries answers too**: the rollup's `countable()` INNER-JOINs `exam_attempts` (for `is_practice`), so deleting the attempt drops its answers from every count by the same silence. `StartAdaptiveSession::discard()` deletes an attempt before any answer exists, which is why it needs no call.
- **A confined assistant manages the homework of their OWN courses only, and every staff door on an assignment asks `AssistantScopeDirectory::mayActOnCourse()` — the list's filter was never the guard (fixed 2026-09-29).** `AssignmentPolicy` asked the workspace and `assignments.manage` and nothing else, so a confined assistant holding a far course's assignment uuid could edit it, publish it, read its marking list, grant an extension on it, mark its submissions (`SubmissionPolicy::grade()`) and open the handed-in files (`SubmissionPolicy::view()`, re-run by `SubmissionFileController` at every open). Now `AssignmentPolicy::manage()` (behind `update`/`delete`/`publish`/`extend`/`submissions`), `view()`, and both `SubmissionPolicy` staff branches ask the scope; `store` and `update` also ask `placeInCourse` for the TARGET course, or an assistant sets homework for a far course — or moves their own there — by naming it. A course-less assignment is refused to a confined assistant (the directory's null branch, the exam-without-a-course answer): it reaches every student of the workspace, the widest ground there is. ⚠️ **In `view()` the scope is asked ABOVE the student branch**, because `StudentScope::permits()`'s first arm allows any reader whose context is the assignment's workspace — which a confined assistant always has — so asked after it, the far course's published homework opened to them through the student door. It is a no-op for students (the directory passes anyone who is not an assistant in that workspace), and a refusal there FALLS THROUGH to the student's own entitlement — the chat rule (`ConversationPolicy::withinStaffScope()`): a confined assistant actively enrolled in the far course reads and hands in its PUBLISHED homework as a student (`submit()` authorises `view`). Only the ENROLMENT arm is asked (`EnrollmentDirectory::activeCourseIdsFor()`), never `StudentScope::permits()`'s context arm, and it grants student powers only — `manage`, the marking list, extensions and grading stay refused; a course-less assignment has no course to be enrolled in and stays refused. ⚠️ `SubmissionPolicy` reads the course with `withoutWorkspaceScope()` (the `AsksAssistantScope::courseOfSession()` idiom): a scoped read that came back empty would be a `null` course and refuse the assistant their own course's work. `AssignmentAssistantScopeTest` walks seven doors × near/far/course-less. The same gap on exams is closed by the entry below.
- **A confined assistant manages the exams of their OWN courses only, and `ExamPolicy` asks `mayActOnCourse()` on every staff ability (fixed 2026-09-29).** `ExamPolicy` asked the workspace and the permission and nothing else (only `AttemptPolicy` and `GradingPolicy` asked the scope), so a confined assistant holding a far course's exam uuid opened its draft, edited it, filled it from the bank and read its answer key (`/manage/exams/{uuid}/items` — `manageQuestions`, which `SyncExamItemsRequest` also asks), published it and deleted it; `GET /exams` listed every paper of the workspace to them; and `POST /exams` / `PUT /exams/{uuid}` put an exam in any course or none. Now `update`, `delete`, `publish` and `manageQuestions` ask the scope after the workspace; `view()` asks it ABOVE the student branch (the `AssignmentPolicy::view()` reason: `StudentScope::permits()`'s context arm lets any member of the workspace through — and `AttemptController::start()` authorises `view`, so a confined assistant could SIT a far course's paper they were never enrolled in); `store` and `update` ask `placeInCourse` for the TARGET course. ⚠️ **`ExamController::update()` called no `authorize()` at all** — `UpdateExamRequest::authorize()` asks `exams.update` and nothing else, so the policy was never consulted on an edit; the controller now asks `update` first. ⚠️ **The target course on an edit is `array_key_exists('course_id', $validated)`, never `?? null`**: `validated()` drops an absent key, so `?? null` reads every title-only edit as a move to no course and refuses it to every confined assistant. A course-less exam is refused to a confined assistant (the directory's null branch). A `view` refusal falls through to an ACTIVE ENROLMENT in the exam's course, published papers only — the assistant sits it as a student and gains no staff power — and the managing list (`ExamController::index`, `scopedCourseIdsFor()`) shows their own courses' exams plus exactly those published papers, so the exam tab of a course they study in is not empty. Not scoped, deliberately: the question bank (`questions` has no course — `bank.view` / `questions.manage` are workspace-wide), item analysis and accommodations (scoped on their own axes since 2026-09-30 — the entry below), `ReferenceTargetController` and `ManageLessons` (already behind `CoursePolicy::manageLessons`, which asks the scope), and Filament (platform staff only). `ExamAssistantScopeTest` walks six doors × near/far/course-less.
- **An exam's course arrives as a UUID (`course`), because no screen has the integer — and a confined assistant could not create an exam at all until 2026-09-30.** `StoreExamRequest`/`UpdateExamRequest` accepted only `course_id`, «اختبار جديد» posted no course, and `ExamPolicy::placeInCourse()` (correctly) refuses a confined assistant a course-less paper — so the regression of #299 was that the only create screen could never succeed for them. Both requests now take `course` through `WorkspaceRules::exists('courses', 'uuid')` (a foreign uuid is a 422 before any policy) and `ExamController::resolveCourse()` turns it into `course_id` BEFORE the scope check and removes it before `create()`/`update()` mass-assign the payload; present `course` wins over `course_id`, an explicit null moves the paper to none, absent leaves it. `course_id` stays for the panel and old clients. The page reads `is_confined_assistant` from `/auth/me` (`scopedCourseIdsFor() !== null`): confined ⇒ the picker is required and «كل طلابي» is not offered; everybody else keeps the course-less paper, which `StudentScope` shows to every student of the workspace. The list is `GET /courses?per_page=200`, already narrowed for a confined assistant. Guard: the uuid cases in `tests/Feature/Assessments/ExamAssistantScopeTest.php`.
- **Item analysis and accommodations are confined too — on their OWN axes, the lesson's course and the student (fixed 2026-09-30).** `analytics.view` is on the DEFAULT assistant role, so this one was live for every assistant: `GET /manage/analytics/concepts` names a lesson on every row (`ConceptStatResource`), i.e. the lesson titles of every course in the workspace, and `/questions` listed every question. For a confined reader `AnalyticsController` now keeps the concept rows whose `lesson_id` is a lesson of their courses — and so drops the «overall» row (`lesson_id = 0`): it names nothing but aggregates EVERY course's attempts and cannot be narrowed — and the questions whose own lesson is in their courses OR that sit in an exam of one; a bank question tied to neither is refused (the directory's null rule). ⚠️ **A shared question's numbers still count every sitting of it**: the rollup is per question, not per course, so a question used in a near and a far exam shows both — splitting it is a change to `RollUpQuestionStatsJob`, not to the read. `accommodations.manage` (not on the default role): the list is `whereIn('student_user_id', scopedStudentIdsFor())`, `GrantAccommodation` asks `mayActOnStudent()` inside the SAME `DomainException` as «not your student» (so the controller's 404 stays one answer — no probe), and `AccommodationPolicy::manage()` asks it for a revoke. ⚠️ **The arrangement is one row per student per workspace and applies to every course** — an in-scope assistant's grant also lengthens that student's exams in courses the assistant does not work on; the student is theirs and the arrangement is about the student. `AnalyticsAssistantScopeTest` · `AccommodationAssistantScopeTest`.
